ABOUT US

Welcome to Café Debuggers Hut!

At Café Debuggers Hut, we're not just about serving great coffee and delicious treats; we're about creating a space where programmers and tech enthusiasts can come together to fuel their creativity and passion for coding.

From our carefully curated menu featuring specialty coffees and homemade pastries to our cozy ambiance designed for deep focus and collaboration, every aspect of our café is crafted with the programmer in mind.

So come on in, grab a seat, and join us on this exciting journey. Whether you're here to debug your code or just to enjoy a moment of peace and quiet, we're thrilled to have you as part of our community.

Cheers,
The Café Debuggers Hut Team
